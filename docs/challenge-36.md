# Challenge #36 — GAIAEZ · Intelligent Mosque Operations (our summary)

Official profile: https://drive.google.com/file/d/196QOtDorcwimEZlIMqfk7AQpIgFCjKQc/view
This is a paraphrase — read the original.

## What they ask
Help mosque operations teams monitor and optimise energy, equipment performance and building services across mosques of different sizes and technical readiness — cutting cost and waste while keeping worshipper comfort, safety and service continuity, using accessible operational data, **smart sensing** and appropriate AI.

## Root causes (how Nabd answers each)
| # | Root cause (paraphrased) | Nabd |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Demand swings with prayer times, seasons and occasions, but controls are static | Worship-calendar baseline |
| R2 | HVAC, lighting, water, maintenance and bills live in separate places | One per-mosque timeline + digest |
| R3 | No low-cost integration path for old or mixed equipment | Clip-on current sensing, no BMS |
| R4 | Teams can't tell abnormal usage from legitimate demand | Waste + fault flags with reasons |
| R5 | Automated actions need override, safety and maintenance rules | Advisory only; technician confirms |

## Hidden ecosystem (from the profile)
- Silent beneficiary: worshippers in older, smaller, under-resourced mosques
- Overlooked intermediary: on-site technicians who know real occupancy and equipment quirks → our Confirm / False-alarm loop captures that knowledge
- Missing enabler: a low-cost evidence-and-control framework with benchmarks, human override and a safe prototype-to-pilot path → our 90-day plan
