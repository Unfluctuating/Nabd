## Q&A prep (one-sentence answers)
- **ADDC/Siemens already did this?** They control; we diagnose — cheaper, covers mosques outside their programme, and can feed their M&V.
- **Who installs it?** A licensed electrician, ~30 min per board; clamps are non-invasive.
- **False alarms?** Technician confirms or rejects every flag; we report the confirmation rate as a KPI.
- **Why not a full BMS?** Cost and old equipment — that's root cause R3.
- **Who pays?** (Answer once confirmed on Day 1.)
- **Is the data real?** Simulated, grounded in the 146-mosque UAE audit study; the pilot replaces it with real data.
